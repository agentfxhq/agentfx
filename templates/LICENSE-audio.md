# Default template audio

`music-visualizer/night-drive.mp3` and `lyric-video/night-drive.mp3` (identical files) are
"Night Drive", a 16 s synthwave groove at 120 bpm (8 bars, A minor, starts on a downbeat), made
for AgentFX on 2026-11-09 with the in-house reel-music pipeline from the score in
`_audio/night-drive.yaml`: analog-modelled synths and a synthesized 808/909 drum kit only, no
samples, no third-party presets, no plugins. Mastered to -14 LUFS, encoded as MP3 160 kbit/s.
