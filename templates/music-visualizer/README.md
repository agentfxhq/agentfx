# Music visualizer

Audio visualizer for any song: upload a track and get a spectrum skyline with a reflection (`bars`), a spinning record ringed by a radial spectrum (`radial`) or an oscilloscope trace over a fine spectrum floor (`waveform`), with title, artist and a progress rule. Every moving part is driven by the music at compile time (8 log bands, level, beat grid, smoothed), so it stays calm on quiet songs and kicks on loud ones. Serves "music visualizer" and "audio visualizer" (4.2k/month each). Default track: `night-drive.mp3` (see `../LICENSE-audio.md`).
